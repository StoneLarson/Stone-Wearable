Breath-hold validation — fingertip, 2026-11-18
===============================================

Purpose: confirm that the respiratory signal in the inter-beat intervals is real,
by removing the breathing and watching it disappear. This check needs no pacing
accuracy at all, which is why it is the strongest single validation available
without reference equipment.

Subject: Stone, index finger, seated.
Protocol: ~15 s of normal breathing, then a breath hold to the end of the record.
Build on the device: ARTIFACT_FRACTION = 0.25 (NOT the 0.45 source on disk —
established after the fact by replaying the logged intervals; see Finding 2).

Record: 41 accepted beats, 37.5 s, mean IBI 932 ms (64.4 BPM).

RESULT — RSA collapses when breathing stops
-------------------------------------------
Lomb-Scargle over the 0.04-0.5 Hz band, 12 s sliding windows:

  window         beats  SD(IBI)   RMSSD    peak      RSA power
  0.0-12.0 s      14    102.5 ms   93.5 ms  3.9/min    52686
  4.0-16.0 s      13     79.6 ms  101.1 ms 12.5/min    25615
  8.0-20.0 s      12     80.7 ms   81.0 ms  7.9/min    27563
  12.0-24.0 s     13     59.7 ms   27.8 ms  6.0/min    16233
  16.0-28.0 s     13     33.1 ms   28.7 ms  6.9/min     4497
  20.0-32.0 s     13     29.2 ms   29.8 ms  2.4/min     4129
  24.0-36.0 s     12     42.1 ms   35.8 ms  3.4/min     9128

  first half (0-18.7 s):   SD 98.4 ms, peak 5.7 breaths/min, power 42510
  second half (18.7-37.5): SD 47.5 ms, peak 3.1 breaths/min, power 13442

RSA power falls 12.8x (52686 -> 4129) and SD(IBI) falls 3.5x (102.5 -> 29.2 ms)
between the breathing section and the hold. The breathing section recovers
5.7 breaths/min, close to a normal resting rate.

PASS. The oscillation in the interval series is driven by breathing.

Two supporting physiological signatures, neither of which was designed for:
  - During the hold, IBI drifts monotonically 900 -> 1050 ms (67 -> 57 BPM).
    Breath-hold bradycardia.
  - On resumption, intervals drop to 618-685 ms (87-97 BPM). Post-hold
    tachycardia. All four of these beats were discarded by the 0.25 band.

Caveats
-------
37.5 s is a short record: ~3.8 respiratory cycles whole, ~2 per segment. The
whole-record peak reads 2.8/min, below the physiological band edge, because the
record mixes two regimes plus the bradycardia drift — segment analysis is the
correct read here, not the whole-record number. The 5.7/min figure rests on about
two cycles and is encouraging rather than conclusive. A 3-minute metronome-paced
run at 6 breaths/min is still needed for a defensible accuracy figure.

FINDING 2 — the board was running a stale build
-----------------------------------------------
The device still had ARTIFACT_FRACTION = 0.25 despite 0.45 being in the source.
Proof from the log alone:
  - 618 ms was rejected. Rejection at 0.45 would require periodEst > 1124 ms;
    periodEst was ~950. At 0.25 it requires > 824 ms, which holds.
  - "Beat IBI: 640.6 ms  BPM: 93.7" — 60000/640.6 = 93.7 exactly, a one-interval
    average, which only happens after MAX_REJECTS flushes the BPM window. That
    needs three consecutive rejections, which 0.45 would not have produced.

Consequence: the four real post-hold beats were thrown away, and RMSSD reset.
A build banner was added to setup() in 2026-11-18b so every future log states
its own tuning constants and this cannot recur silently.
